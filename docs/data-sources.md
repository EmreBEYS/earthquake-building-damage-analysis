## Source Review Checklist

Before adding a dataset to `data/raw/`, confirm that:

- the original download URL and access date are recorded;
- the license or terms of use permit the intended use;
- redistribution restrictions are understood;
- geographic and temporal coverage match the study scope;
- variable and damage-label definitions are available;
- personal, sensitive, or restricted information is not committed;
- the original file is preserved unchanged and its checksum is recorded.
